A Sample Post Written for the Site

This is a short example of a post that lives directly on the blog. It is useful for notes that should feel separate from a repository: project reflections, implementation writeups, reading notes, or anything that benefits from a calmer reading layout.

Why keep some posts local?

A local post gives you control over the presentation. You can keep the writing concise, link to supporting code when it matters, and avoid making every article feel like repository documentation.

External markdown links still make sense for design notes that belong beside the code. The blog can point to those directly while keeping lighter writing here.

Example shape

A good local post can be simple:

  • one clear idea
  • a few paragraphs of context
  • links to code or references when useful
  • a short conclusion or next step

The goal is not to make the site complicated. It is to give each piece of writing the right home.
